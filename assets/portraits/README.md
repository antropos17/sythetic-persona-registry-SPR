# Fictional passport portraits

These eight portraits were generated for the SPR demonstration on 19 October 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. Each depicts a fictional adult. They are illustrative assets, not photographs of applicants or evidence of identity, consent, rights, or an examiner's review. Do not present them as official identity documents.

The shared generation brief requested an original adult, a centered front-facing head and shoulders, direct eye contact, a neutral closed-mouth expression, a matte light gray background, diffuse light, ordinary clothing, and photographic skin and hair texture. It excluded text, watermarks, document borders, flags, glamour retouching, dramatic lighting, and CGI styling. One generation call was made for each persona; the selected PNG output was encoded as a quality-90 JPEG for the site without retouching or compositing.
